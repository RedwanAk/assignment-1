    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is DevConf 2026?

    DevConf 2026 is a conference for developers, designers, and technology enthusiasts to learn from industry experts, discover the latest trends, and connect with the tech community.

    Can I upgrade my ticket later?

    Yes! You can upgrade your Standard ticket to Pro or Team tier anytime before August 10 through your account dashboard.

    Who can attend the event?

    Anyone interested in technology is welcome, including students, software developers, engineers, designers, entrepreneurs, and tech professionals of all experience levels.

    What is included with my ticket?

    Depending on your ticket type, you'll receive access to conference sessions, keynote presentations, networking opportunities, and other event benefits as outlined in your selected package.
